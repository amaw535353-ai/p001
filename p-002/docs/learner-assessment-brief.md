authorization and scope for the named disposable lab before testing it. Explain the
business context, inventory identities and assets, distinguish intended policy from
observed behavior, independently choose relevant hypotheses and collect evidence.
Do not infer a finding from an intentionally inconsistent fixture or an oracle verdict.

The builder supplies a coherent data factory, safe scenarios, measured engineering
tests and compatibility limitations. You own hypothesis selection, boundary testing,
false-positive elimination, impact analysis, mitigation review, regressions and oral
consultancy defense. No sample finding is pre-completed. No external scanning or
production credentials are authorized by the fixture catalog. All four workbook gates
start NOT DEMONSTRATED; AI review can critique evidence but cannot certify independence.
