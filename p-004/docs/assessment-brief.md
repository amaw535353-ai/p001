# Human assessor brief

Mission: reconstruct a bounded synthetic AI application architecture, distinguish
declarations from observed behavior, and prepare defensible discovery/scoping input.
Choose explicit files and, only with independently reviewed local approval, fixed
read-only target paths. Record what remains unseen and why. Do not run exploitation,
active scanners, fuzzing, form submissions or arbitrary third-party traffic.

Review tenant/user/workload/service identity separation, API contracts, data-store
links, potential tool/connector entry points and candidate crossings. Every claim
needs source/decision evidence and a verification status. Report drift as an
inventory discrepancy. A passive alert or exposed route requires independent human
assessment before it could become a finding.

Deliver a source-backed diagram, coverage/unknowns table, independent reproduction,
modified-architecture comparison and concise consultancy scoping memo. G1–G4 remain
NOT DEMONSTRATED until independent evidence is supplied and reviewed by a qualified
human. AI output is not that evidence.
