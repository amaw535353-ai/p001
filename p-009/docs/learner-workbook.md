# Learner workbook — questions and tasks only

Codex implemented; Codex tested. **Human independently reproduced: NOT DEMONSTRATED.**
**External human reviewer verified: NOT DEMONSTRATED.** G1–G5 remain NOT DEMONSTRATED until
the learner personally performs and defends the work. Do not treat engineering results as answers.

## G1 — Telemetry architecture

Draw the executed OpenLLMetry, Collector, Langfuse, Wazuh and P009 paths. Identify each identity,
trust boundary, credential, source, queue, persistent store, failure mode and unverified edge.
Which claims can the actual native evidence support, and what further measurements are required?

## G2 — Trace and event correlation

Reconstruct an independent synthetic request from exact request, trace, span, tool, decision and
alert identifiers. Defend every association. Investigate ambiguity, gaps, duplicates, clock skew,
out-of-order arrival and P006's absent request ID. What alternative explanations remain possible?

## G3 — Evidence integrity and privacy

Independently plant source canaries and examine each export boundary. Test safe-path refusal,
tampering, manifest consistency, access denial, retention and deletion. Which bytes remain, and
what do local hashes prove about source authenticity, privileged rewrites and time?

## G4 — Security monitoring

Choose a Wazuh rule and independently execute true-positive, benign, false-positive and
false-negative investigations. Explain the preconditions, trusted severity and source reference.
What evidence would be needed before describing the alert as a validated security finding?

## G5 — Consultancy evidence defense

Present scope, source inventory, collection, correlation, redaction, integrity, limitations,
reproducibility and recommendations. Distinguish your own observations from Codex's evidence.
Defend the remaining native product, production and independent verification gates.
