## Self-assessment gates
| Gate | Initial status |
|---|---|
| G1 Guided understanding: explain services, identities, flows and boundaries | NOT DEMONSTRATED |
| G2 Independent reproduction: rebuild and reproduce successful/rejected operations | NOT DEMONSTRATED |
| G3 Unfamiliar implementation: assess changed deployment without builder solution | NOT DEMONSTRATED |
| G4 Consultancy delivery: defend scope, evidence, impact, remediation and retest | NOT DEMONSTRATED |

Questions / small change requests (supply your own evidence, not guessed answers):
1. Reconstruct a request's path and identify every identity transition.
2. Distinguish three controls that operate at different trust boundaries.
3. Explain which evidence supports segmentation and which evidence does not.
4. Design a bounded test for a changed tenant namespace without changing scope.
5. Propose a resource-budget change and demonstrate its measured effect.
6. Trace a deployed artifact to its pinned source and integrity check.
7. Reproduce a safe recovery exercise and account for every removed resource.
8. Identify which cloud claims require a separate genuine AWS engagement.
9. Defend a false-positive decision with reproducible observations.

AI may critique evidence later; it cannot certify independent human competency.
