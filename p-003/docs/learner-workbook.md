# Independent learner workbook — no answer key

SYNTHETIC TRAINING ENGAGEMENT — NOT AUTHORIZATION FOR REAL-WORLD TESTING

Codex built and tested software mechanics. No human competency, professional finding,
legal authority or employment readiness has been demonstrated. G1–G4 remain
NOT DEMONSTRATED until independent human evidence and review are supplied.

1. Draw the trust boundaries between client claims, reviewer identities, review
   evidence, scope compilation, export files, Redmine and future execution tools.
2. Explain how target selection, activity, window, exception, stop event and evidence
   policy interact. Identify which prerequisites are caller assertions.
3. Create unfamiliar fictional fixtures and independently reproduce a positive and
   negative preflight decision without copying the solved engineering fixtures.
4. Review a scope amendment, revocation, out-of-scope asset and an approval-chain
   discrepancy. Record your own methods, evidence and limitations.
5. Identify an assumption this tool cannot technically verify. Propose an appropriate
   human verification control and defend why it addresses that gap.
6. Draft a fictional client-facing ROE and defend its restrictions, evidence handling,
   exclusions and escalation plan. Do not treat generated terms as legal approval.
7. Ask a qualified independent human practitioner to challenge one result when
   possible. Another AI model is not human verification.
