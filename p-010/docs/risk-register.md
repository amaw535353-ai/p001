# Risk register and accountable treatment

Each entry links the exact source taxonomy version and retains its definition
and provenance. The approved P-005 catalog contains 37 slots with 23 sourced
definitions. Unresolved slots permit UNKNOWN or NEEDS_INFORMATION. APPLICABLE
and NOT_APPLICABLE require supporting context and a reviewer rationale.

Record inherent risk, current controls, residual risk and an accountable owner.
Treatment may mitigate, avoid, transfer/share or accept. Acceptance is a
separate attributable decision against one exact risk version and specific
asset references, rationale, constraints, source and review/expiry time.
Expired decisions remain available in history. The demonstration uses
fictional actor labels and simulated decisions.
