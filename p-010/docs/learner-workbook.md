# Independent learner workbook

G1–G8 remain NOT DEMONSTRATED. Provide your own evidence and defend your
answers. Attribute implementation and testing to the appropriate actor.

## G1 — Finding validation

- Which observations support a finding? Which alternatives or missing
  evidence change your conclusion?
- Review a candidate and record your independent disposition.

## G2 — Severity and risk rationale

- How do impact, exploitability, likelihood, confidence and evidence
  quality change your severity rationale?
- Record the rubric version, inputs, uncertainty and any override.

## G3 — Risk register and treatment

- Which source risks apply and which remain unresolved?
- Link controls and owners, then distinguish recommended treatment
  from an accountable owner's decision.

## G4 — Framework mapping

- Which exact controls, requirements or ATLAS techniques apply?
- Cite catalog versions and explain the limits of each evidence link.

## G5 — Consultancy report authorship

- Write and defend an executive summary and technical finding.
- Identify observed facts, source declarations, hypotheses, judgments
  and untested coverage.

## G6 — Retest and residual risk

- Compare two original source versions and their configurations.
- Record comparability, regression checks and residual risk.

## G7 — OSCAL and Redmine

- What source records are required for the selected OSCAL model?
- Verify the local synthetic Redmine binding and demonstrate conflict
  handling with a separate fixture operator.

## G8 — Client-style defense

- Present your scope, methods, evidence, severity, remediation,
  residual risk, limitations and review status to a reviewer.
- Defend each consequential claim and explain any unresolved gate.
